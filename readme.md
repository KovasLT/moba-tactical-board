**MOBA Tactical Board**
This is the community tool to help Honor of Kings community teams to prepare tactical plans in easier way. Heroes and maps belong to Honor of Kings we do not claim any rights to them. This project only provides the license to Index.html file which is the scrip handling heroes placement and ploting.

More information is available on our discord page:
https://discord.gg/DfW5kPKsv5

Counters and Synergies are gathered from publicly available data of 2025-07 -- 2026-07 pro teams matches. Some deviation might appear do to patches. At that time Pei, Chicha and Guan Yu were dominating in the META.
