How to use

Move – drag tokens, notes, text.
Arrow – straight line with head.
Path – straight line (no head).
Circle – gather spot / area.
Sticky Note – click map to drop one.
Text Box – transparent note that grows with its text. Double click to edit.
Compare – click two heroes to see their counter / synergy win rate.
Erase – click any shape, token or note.

⚙️ Generate data.js

Select all hero images from your HEROES/ folder, and map images from MAPS/. The generator reads filenames and produces a data.js file. Drop it into your repo root and the Tactics Assistant will pick it up automatically on next load.
